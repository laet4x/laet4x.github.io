01

OSIF

Active

Open-source OSINT Framework (OSIF) — a reconnaissance platform with a Metasploit-like interface, built to make open-source intelligence accessible to security researchers. Nine modules cover attack-surface mapping, blockchain analysis, email investigation, geolocation, host discovery, IoC tracking, mobile research, social media intelligence, and web property enumeration.

02

Vanguard VDP

Active

A structured vulnerability disclosure program for the laet4x.com domain. Researchers submit findings through an AES-256 encrypted channel with no email required, get acknowledged within 48 hours, and see triage completed within 7 days — with qualified reports featured on a public Hall of Fame.

VDP Responsible Disclosure Security
Visit Vanguard Source
03

Better Aklan

Active

A community-led, open-source portal making the government of the Province of Aklan accessible, transparent, and user-friendly — a municipal-focused fork of BetterGov.PH. Covers a public services directory, a legislative portal for ordinances and resolutions, a transparency dashboard, and a government directory, in English and Filipino.

Civic Tech Open Source Government
